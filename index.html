3D Reconstruction from FPV Flight Data — S.P.L.O.O.G.E

Aug 2026 Python, COLMAP, Rust, WebGL
  • Built a pipeline from raw DJI O4 Pro video and gyro telemetry to one 3D Gaussian Splatting model of two flights six days apart: 7,470 of 7,471 frames registered at 0.82 px mean reprojection error.
  • Estimated the uncalibrated lens focal length by regressing optical flow against gyro rotation (R² = 0.967), and cut video-to-render misalignment from 72.3 px to 0.89 px by undistorting the footage.
  • Confirmed 13,482 verified image matches linked the two sites before committing to an 18-hour mapping run.
  • Traced a rendering defect to the viewer's near-plane cull after measuring four other fixes, including a depth-distortion loss I implemented in Rust. Holes fell from 51.2% to 4.4%, with no regressions across 56 test poses.
